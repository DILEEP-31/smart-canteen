DBMS Workflow: sp_complete_order

When you click Mark as Delivered, the Flask app calls sp_complete_order(p_order_id, OUT ...):

  1. Acquires counter lock on token_slots row 1 to guarantee concurrency safety.
  2. Updates order status to COMPLETED with current timestamp.
  3. Frees up the slot in token_slots.
  4. Finds the oldest record in waiting_list (FIFO order).
  5. If a customer is waiting, assigns the freed token, changes order to ACTIVE, and marks queue entry as PROMOTED.
